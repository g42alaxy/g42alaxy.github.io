## Bio

I'm Matvei Anoshin — an M1 ICFP student at ENS Ulm 🇫🇷, and a BSc in Quantum Computing from MIPT (cum laude). Over 5 years I've built research experience across Machine Learning and Quantum Computing ✨, through international collaborations and summer schools including SLT-2026, EEML-2025, AIRI-2025 and SMILES-2024. I'm a Gold Medalist 🥇 of the All-Russian Student Physicists' Tournament 2025 and a Semifinalist of the International Physicists' Tournament 2024, focused on AI research for Quantum.
